Warcry: Body Arena

Prototype: one mechanic. Throw a spear at the enemy and dodge the ones thrown back.

Webcam: raise a hand above your shoulder for 0.3s to summon a spear, then swing your arm down and forward to throw. For the chakra, bend one arm with the elbow down and your hand out to the side and up, like a sideways V, hold 0.35s, then sweep or snap to throw. Lean, sidestep or duck to dodge. For the energy wave, cup both hands together at your hip off to one side, hold to charge (up to 1.6s) and then thrust or drop them to fire. Cross your arms over your chest (hands on opposite shoulders) and keep them there to hold a shield up. Uncross and it disappears. Stand 2 m back with your upper body and head in view.

Mouse and keyboard: hold the mouse button to summon, release to throw toward the cursor. Hold right mouse (or E) for the chakra. A/D moves, S ducks. Hold F or Shift for the shield. Hold Q to charge the energy wave and release to fire.
